## The answer to "can this be used?"

**Yes — but only for one kind of claim, and it is not the claim the site currently makes.**

**What this data can support:**
- Brokerage-level market share on Smith Lake for calendar 2025.
- "Lake Homes Realty was the #1 brokerage on Smith Lake in 2025 by both transaction sides (44.3%) and dollar volume (51.8%)."
- Total market context: roughly **704 sides** and **~$474.9M** in closed volume across all firms.
- Concentration: the top 2 firms did **70.7%** of sides and **75.0%** of volume.

**What it cannot support:**
- Any statement that Justin Dyar personally is #1, #2 or ranked at all. His name does not appear in this report. Agent-level ranking needs a different MLS report.
- Any per-agent production figures.

**The thing to flag on the call:** the #1 firm in this report, **Lake Homes Realty, is Justin's own
brokerage.** So publishing this on the site means an "independent" publication publishing market data
that places the ranked agent's own firm first — using data supplied by that agent.

That does not make the data unusable. It makes the disclosure question **more** urgent, not less. Get
one of the two fixes agreed first (separate contact details for the publication, or disclose the
relationship), then this data is genuinely good material.
